The Vision

What we could create here

Here is the exciting part. With the shape of the land understood, we can start placing the things that would make this property truly special. Everything sits where it makes the most of the views and the natural slope, including walkout basements that open right onto the hillside.

Your home, at the heart of it all

The residence already on the land stays as the centerpiece, the warm anchor that everything else is built around.

Two larger homes

Set on the higher ground for the very best views, with room for family to have their own space.

A handful of smaller homes

Cozy, welcoming places for guests, friends or family to stay close by.

A shop and office

A dedicated place to work, meet and look after the day to day of the property.

Workshop and craft spaces

Plenty of room to build, make and tinker, for hobbies and hands on projects.

A gathering pavilion

A friendly central spot where everyone can come together for meals, events and good company.

A pool and activity area

Somewhere to relax, play and soak up long summer days right at the center of it all.

Walkout basements and river views

By following the natural slope, homes can step down with the land so lower floors open straight outside, with windows pointed toward the river to the southeast.
